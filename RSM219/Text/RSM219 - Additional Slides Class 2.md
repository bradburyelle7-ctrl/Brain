Gross Margin
Ratio that determines the % of every dollar of revenue retained by the 
company after deducting the direct costs of producing / delivering the 
product / service. 
(Revenue – COGS)
Revenues
Apple Gross Margin – Products
FY 2025 : ($307,003 - 194,116) / 307,003 = 36.77%
FY 2024 : ($294,866 - 185,233) / 294,866 = 37.18%
Apple Gross Margin – Services
FY 2025 : ($109,158 – 26,844) / 109,158 = 75.41%
FY 2024 : ($96,169 - 25,119) / 96,169 = 73.88%

What’s an audit and why should I care?
• Auditors review transactions and internal 
controls of company.

are free from material misstatements
