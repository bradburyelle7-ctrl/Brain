EXERCISE: TOTAL HEALTH LTD. (THL)  – JOURNAL ENTRIES  
 
July Transactions 
 
Ref. Description 
1.1 On July 1, investors contributed $10,000 in cash to start Total Health Ltd. (THL). In exchange, they received 
500 common shares. 
1.2 On July 15, THL purchased $3,000 of inventory “on account”, agreeing to pay the supplier within 30 days.  
1.3 On July 31, Total Health Ltd. (THL) purchased equipment costing $6,000 and paid cash. The equipment is 
expected to be used for 5 years and have no residual value at the end of that time.  
1.4 To help finance the equipment purchase, THL borrowed $6,000 on July 31. THL will pay back the loan after five 
years. In addition, THL must pay monthly interest of $50. The first interest payment must be made on 
September 1, and it covers the interest relating to the month of August.

August Transactions and Adjusting Entries 
 
Ref. Description 
2.1 On August 1, THL signed a rental agreement for a small pop-up shop in a busy mall. The agreement calls for monthly payments of 
$1,000 at the start of each month. The first payment was made at the time of signing.  
2.2 Rental agreement (described in 2.1 above) also required a refundable damage deposit of $1,500. Deposit was also paid at signi ng. 
2.3 On August 15, THL paid for all inventory purchased on July 15 (inventory originally had a cost of $3,000, and none of it was paid at the 
time of purchase). 
2.4 On August 20, THL paid $1,000 for an advertisement that appeared in the local newspaper on August 25.  
2.5 During the month of August, THL made retail sales of $8,500. The cost of inventory relating to these sales was $2,200. All sa les were 
made by cash or credit card, with $300 of credit card sales still outstanding on August 31.  
2.6 At the end of August, THL paid $3,000 in wages to the various part-time staff working in the store during the month of August. There were 
no unpaid wages at the end of August. 
2.7 At the end of August, THL was able to secure a corporate order for promotional wellness products. The customer put down a $1, 000 
deposit and will pay the remaining $1,500 when products are delivered on September 10 . 
2.8 On August 31, THL declared and paid out $1,000 in dividends.
