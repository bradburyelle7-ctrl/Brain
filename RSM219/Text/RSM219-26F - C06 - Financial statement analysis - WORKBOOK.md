We use financial ratios to help diagnose financial health 
 Financial ratios help us relate items on the financial statements in a meaningful and comparable 
way 
 Definitions of the ratios may vary depending on the purpose of the analysis  
 Financial ratios are generally used to identify key questions for further analysis – rather than 
providing deep insight by themselves  
 
 
Our focus is on financial 
reports including financial 
statements (and notes)  
Adjust financial statements, prepare common-size 
and trend analysis, prepare ratios (and graphs).   
Focus on analysis rather than computation. Link the numbers to the 
company’s strategy and business environment. Go beyond describing and 
explain reasons for performance, risks, and implications for the future.    
Conclusions and 
recommendations must 
tie back to the purpose 
of the analysis. 
 Identifies key economic relationships (e.g., gross profit to 
revenues, earnings to assets, etc.) 
 Comparability of financial performance over time (scaled) 
 Comparability with other companies (or industry 
benchmarks) 
 Easy access through data aggregators (e.g., Capital IQ, 
FactSet, Bloomberg, etc.) – but be careful! 
❑ Difficult to find “comparable” companies (or even clearly 
identify an industry/sector for some companies). Consider 
industry-specific ratios but be aware of ratio definitions! 
❑ Irregular items can skew ratios and need to be carefully 
considered (and, adjusted) 
❑ Different companies may use different accounting standards 
and judgments (e.g., IFRS vs. US GAAP), making it more 
difficult to compare ratios.

EXERCISE 1.2: Amazon (AMZN) 
Analyze the selected financial statements and segment disclosures of Amazon (see Appendix 1). Recall our 
previous analysis of Amazon and the following key points:  
 Revenues rose 12% to $717 billion in 2025, and gross profit margins expanded to 50.3%.  
 The swing in "other income (expense), net" continues to add volatility to earnings: this line moved from a 
$2.3 billion expense in 2024 to a $15.2 billion gain in 2025. This relates to fair value measurement of 
Amazon’s investment in Rivian. Net income grew 31% to $77.7 billion, helped by this non-operating swing 
on top of stronger operating performance. 
 On the balance sheet, retained earnings again increased by exactly the amount of net income (from 
$172,866M to $250,536M, a $77,670M increase matching 2025 net income), confirming Amazon continues 
to retain all its earnings. Goodwill was essentially unchanged at $23.3 billion (vs. $23.1 billion in 2024), 
suggesting limited acquisition activity.

#2 | We can use DuPont analysis to understand company performance  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
DuPont analysis and integrative ratios 
It is critical to understand the relationships between various ratios. DuPont analysis allows us to decompose 
an important profitability measure: return on equity (ROE). By breaking it down, we can understand the 
drivers of ROE (over time and compared to other companies).
