  These practice questions are designed to help improve your understanding of the course material. The questions 
referenced in this document can be found at the end of each chapter in your textbook.  
 
Use the Table of Contents below to identify the list of questions and the suggested time for completing each one. When 
using this resource: 
❑ Do the exercises soon after the lecture and ideally before tutorials. Studies on learning show that practicing 
material shortly after it’s introduced improves understanding and retention. Spacing out your practice and reviewing 
regularly also strengthens long-term memory. 
❑ Attempt the exercises first without looking at the solutions or debriefing notes. This will help you simulate real exam 
conditions and identify areas where you may need further practice. 
❑ Be conscious of the suggested time for each question. Staying aware of time management is a valuable skill for 
exams. 
❑ After completing the exercises, use the solutions provided to debrief your work. Compare your answers, understand 
any mistakes, and learn from them to solidify your understanding of the concepts.

Philips (€ millions) Amount Classification 
Net sales .........................................................................................  € 19,482 I 
Income tax expense ........................................................................  337 I 
Retained earnings ...........................................................................  8,296 B 
Net earnings ....................................................................................  1,173 I 
Property, plant and equipment (net) .......................................  2,866 B 
General and admin expense ...........................................................  631 I 
Accounts receivable ........................................................................  4,554 B 
Total liabilities .................................................................................  14,391 B 
Equity ..............................................................................................  12,625 B 
 
b. Total assets = Total liabilities + Total equity 
 Total assets =       €14,391+      €12,625 = €27,016 
 
 Net Sales – Total Expenses = Net Income 
 €19,482 – Total Expenses = €1,173;  Thus, Total Expenses = €18,309 
 
 
c. Return on equity  = Net income/ Avg. total equity  
  = €1,173 / €12,625 = 0.093 or 9.3% 
 ROE is an estimate because we have only this year’s equity for the denominator. 
 
 Debt-to-equity ratio  = Total liabilities / Total equity 
  = €14,391 / €12,625 = 1.14
