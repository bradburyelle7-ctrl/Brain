--- Slide 3 ---
Accrual Accounting: Income and Expenses are recorded as they are earned / incurred.
Income (Revenue / Sales) → Increase in assets or a decrease in liabilities
Revenues are incurred when a resources is earned
Expenses → Decrease in assets or increase in liabilities
Expenses are incurred when a resource is consumed.
*Remember that contributions to/from holders of equity claims are not considered income or expenses.
Accrual vs. Cash Accounting
Cash Accounting:
Cash Inflows: Recognized when company receives cash
Cash Outflows: Recognized when company pays cash
On Friday Sept 20th, you go out for a dinner that costs $200.   You paid with your credit card. Your credit card has to be paid on October 10th.
Definition of Accrual: the accumulation or increase of something over time.
