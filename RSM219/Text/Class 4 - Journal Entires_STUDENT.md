What is a Journal Entry?
DATE Account & Explanation Debit Credit
1. The Date captures the timing of the transaction or adjustment.  This is important since financial 
statements are prepared at a specific dates.  This helps to determine if a transaction should be 
included in the FS for a given period. 
2. Accounts will help us identify which financial elements are impacted by the transactions. 
3. The explanation will help to summarize some key details of the transaction in case any questions arise 
or if it needs to be reviewed or audited later on. 
4. The Debits will always be on the left while the credits will always be on the right.  Debits and Credits 
will capture the $ movement in the specific account.  A Debit and Credit will signal whether the 
amount is increasing or decreaing the balance of a specific Financial Statement without needing to 
use + / -.
5. For any transaction, all debits must equal the credits.
