Q1. We have buyers and sellers of used Nintendo switches with the Willingness to Pay (WTP) and Willingness to
Sell (WTS) for each given in the table below. Each buyer (and seller) buy (sell) only 1 unit if they buy (or
sell).
What will be the change in Consumer Surplus if market price changes from $20 to $30?
Buyer WTP Seller WTS
A $34 W $5
B $17 X $20
C $53 Y $17
D $23 Z $40
A. −$30
B. −$23
C. −$20
D. −$13
E. −$10
