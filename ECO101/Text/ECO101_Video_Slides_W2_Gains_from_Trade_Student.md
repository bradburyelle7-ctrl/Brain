Comparative Advantage (CA)
Deﬁnition: Individual person/ﬁrm/country has a
comparative advantage in a good/activity if the
opportunity cost (OC) of producing the good is lower
for that individual compared to others.

Deﬁnitions
PPF: Production Possibility Frontier
• Feasible: Output combinations that are possible to
produce given available resources.
• Eﬃcient: The only way to get more of one good is
to get less of the other.
• PPF: All output combinations where inputs are used
eﬃciently to produce output

Summary
OC: Opportunity Cost
• Comparative advantage diﬀerences ⇒ gains from
trade.
• If society is producing a good eﬃciently, it should
• From among the people able to switch resources into
producing that good, choose to person with the lowest
OC of production.
• Put them into production.
• Their OC is the society wide OC when producing
eﬃciently.
