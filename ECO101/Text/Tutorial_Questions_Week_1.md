Q1. Mei paid $15 for a non-refundable ticket for a 2-hour long movie. She could sell this ticket for $10. If she
doesn’t go to the movie, she could work for 2 hours and earn $50 (total). Based on this information, if Mei
chooses work over the movie, her value from watching the movie is at most $_____.
A. $24.99
B. $39.99
C. $49.99
D. $59.99
E. $64.99
Q2. Kiki runs a delivery service between 4-8pm only. She does exactly one delivery per hour. Every delivery
requires crossing a bridge for which she has to pay a toll fee that depends on the hour she makes the delivery,
summarized in the table below. She charges her customers a per-delivery toll surcharge of$p. Focusing only
on toll fees and surcharges, what is the lowestp can be for Kiki to be willing to make exactly two deliveries
every evening?
Hour 4-5pm 5-6pm 6-7pm 7-8pm
per-delivery toll fee $7 $12 $10 $5
A. $5
B. $7
C. $10
D. $12
E. None of the options above.
Q3. Zuko has a monthly cookie subscription. For$7 a month, he can order as many cookies as he wants paying$3
per cookie. His marginal beneﬁt from theQth cookie is M B(Q) = 10 − 2Q. The subscription fee increases,
but the per-cookie price decreases. Assuming he continues to buy the subscription after the price change,
each month he buys ____ cookies and his Net Beneﬁt from the subscription _____.
A. more; decreases.
B. more; could increase or decrease.
C. more; increases.
D. fewer; increases.
E. fewer; decreases.
Q4. You need to buy a bike. You have narrowed it down to two options — a blue bike and a red bike. Suppose
the price of the RED bike decreases. Your Opportunity Cost of buying the BLUE bike ____ and your
Willingness to Pay for the BLUE bike ____.
A. increases; increases
B. increases; decreases
C. increases; stays the same.
D. decreases; increases
E. decreases; decreases
