Assumption - No Externalities
MWTP: Marginal Willingness to Pay, MC: Marginal Cost
• Ben (and only Ben) gets all the beneﬁts from the
transferred good.
• Marginal Social Beneﬁt = Marginal Private Beneﬁt
(= MWTP)
• All the costs of producing and giving up the good
are borne by Sue (and only Sue)
• Marginal Social Cost = Marginal Private Cost ( = MC )
• This is not the case for many cases, e.g. pollution
from gasoline

Eﬃciency
MSC: Marginal Social Cost, MSB: Marginal Social Beneﬁt
An allocation is eﬃcient if
• It maximizes (Societal) Total Surplus ⇒ produce
and trade all goods where MSB ≥ MSC and not
any others
• No way to make at least one person better oﬀ
without making anyone worse oﬀ.
