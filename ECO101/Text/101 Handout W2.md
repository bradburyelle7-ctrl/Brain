Summary
ECO101: Principles of Microeconomics, Prof. Freitas 11
• Absolute advantage: who can produce more 
with 1 unit of the resource
• Comparative advantage: who gives up less of 
the other good to produce 1 unit
• Opportunity Cost (OC) captures production 
trade-offs from reallocating resources
• OC depends on relative productivity
• Expressed in units of the other good
• Only relevant when all resources are being 
put to use efficiently

Trade if…
• …What you’re offered is at least as good as your 
next best option
• Here: Next best option is doing it yourself at your 
Opp. Cost of production
• Sell if you’re offered at minimum your OC of 
producing it
• Buy if you’re paying at most your OC of producing it 
yourself.

Two people, two goods
Typical questions
• How to allocate people’s finite resources to good 
production?
• Is this allocation efficient? 
• Efficient if you cannot get more of (at least) one good 
without giving up some of the other good
• PPF: Production Possibility Frontier
• Helps us answer the questions quickly using a graph

Joint PPF: Working together
PPF: Production Possibility Frontier
• Points on the boundary: maximum that can be 
produced with the given resources
Process:
• Start with everyone’s resources in one good
• Produce the first unit of other good by moving 
the lowest cost producer into its production
• If they run out of time, switch to the next lowest 
cost person. Continue. 
• Stop when everyone has all their time in the 
other good’s production
