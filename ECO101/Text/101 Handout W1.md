Three Principles
P1:  Always use Opportunity Costs
• Set the choice up as Benefits vs. Opportunity 
Cost
• The next best alternative use of resources is 
built into Opp. Cost
P2:  If I choose it, what quantity?
• Compare marginal (extra) opportunity cost and 
marginal benefit
P3:  That quantity, or nothing?
• Compare total benefits and total opportunity 
cost at that quantity

--- Page 10 ---
Why Opportunity Cost?
• Choice means allocating scarce resources
• The cost of doing something is giving up the 
chance to put those resources to their next best 
use
• What if a resource has no alternative use? 
Or cannot be reallocated to another use?
• Then it is sunk. It is not a relevant cost for 
your decision

Calculating Opportunity Cost
• Identify the resources being used
• Identify the next best alternative (NBA) use of 
the resources
ECO101: Principles of Microeconomics, Prof. Freitas 11
Resources used: what you must give up to do the thing
$V(NBA): the value from putting the resources to their next best alternative
$Resources(NBA): what resources the alternative would itself have used up
$OC  =  $Resources used   +   $V(NBA)   –
$Resources(NBA)
