Scandal

Arthur Anderson, Enron’s auditor 
to later become Chief Accounting 
Officer at Enron

purpose entities (SPEs) and small 
partnerships such as ChewCo used 
to hide debt and inflate earnings

launches an inquiry of Enron’s

Post-Enron Implications
• “The Big 5” Accounting firms became 
“The Big 4” after demise of Arthur 
Andersen
• Creditors owed more than $63 billion by 
Enron have to settle for about 1/5 of that 
sum
• 5,600 people lose their jobs and $2 
billion pension
• U.S. Congress is forced to pass new 
accounting regulation under the 
Sarbanes-Oxley Act which encompass 
much stricter rules and greater 
accountability by management (Bill 198 
aka CSOX in Canada)
