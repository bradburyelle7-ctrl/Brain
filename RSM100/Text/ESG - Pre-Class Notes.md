--- Slide 57 ---
A Fraud of Epic Proportions
--- Slide 58 ---
ENRON Background
Enron was created in 1985 with the formation  of two large energy companies:  Houston Natural Gas and InterNorth
Kenneth Lay served as CEO throughout except in 2001 when Jeffrey Skilling, Chief Operating Officer at Enron held the title briefly for 8 months
Enron was the 7th largest company in the U.S. when it declared bankruptcy in 2001
--- Slide 59 ---
How the Accounting Scandal was Carried Out
Accountant Richard Causey leaves Arthur Anderson, Enron’s auditor to later become Chief Accounting Officer at Enron
Starting in 1997, Enron begins creating off balance sheet special purpose entities (SPEs) and small partnerships such as ChewCo used to hide debt and inflate earnings
--- Slide 60 ---
Unable to Cover Up the Mess
The “shady” accounting practices threaten to ruin the company and Enron is forced to declare massive losses in its 2001 3rd quarter results followed by a series of restatements of the financial statements for the past 5 years
The SEC becomes suspicious and launches an inquiry of Enron’s finances

--- Slide 66 ---
Post-Enron Implications
“The Big 5” Accounting firms became “The Big 4” after demise of Arthur Andersen
Creditors owed more than $63 billion by Enron have to settle for about 1/5 of that sum
5,600 people lose their jobs and $2 billion pension
U.S. Congress is forced to pass new accounting regulation under the Sarbanes-Oxley Act which encompass much stricter rules and greater accountability by management (Bill 198 aka CSOX in Canada)
--- Slide 67 ---
Where Are They Now…
Kenneth Lay (former CEO) and his protégé, Jeffrey Skilling, are found guilty of fraud and deceit on May 25, 2006
Lay (64) dies of heart attack six weeks later
Skilling (61) is currently serving his 24 year sentence
--- Slide 68 ---
Where Are They Now…
Andrew Fastow (CFO) served 6 years in prison
Received this reduced sentence in part for his cooperation in the trials against Lay and Skilling
Lea Fastow (Andy Fastow’s wife) served 1 year in prison for her involvement (filing false income tax returns)
The prospect of her sentencing led to Andy’s cooperation in return for joint plea agreements
