Most strategies fail, but why?
1. Lack of clear vision 
2. Poor execution
3. Resistance to change
4. Insufficient resources/skills/capabilities
5. Failure to adapt to changing conditions  
6. Weak performance measurement 
7. Too much at once

Key Takeaways
• Always keep an open-mind along your career journey​.
• Organizations grow in predictable phases​, leadership teams need to be fit-for-size.
• Strategic context and organizational readiness is very important.
• Strategies fail – learn from the failures​.
• Execution requires leaders to build and tend to the team, and implement a 
disciplined management model, all while capturing the hearts and minds of the 
Company.​
• … always be reading, networking and most importantly delivering results. ​
