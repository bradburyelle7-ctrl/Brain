RSM100: PACE
Project and Career Supports for You
Why does this matter?
Students consistently tell us they want more opportunities to 
explore careers and develop professional skills early in their 
degree. The PACE modules help you connect what you're 
learning in the classroom with your future academic and career 
decisions.
Career Exploration Focus
• Complete the CareerLeader® Assessment to better 
understand your interests, motivations, and strengths
• Learn practical networking strategies and apply them 
through Rotman Commerce networking opportunities
Support for your RSM100 Business Plan Project
• Work effectively in your project teams
• Effectively cite, incorporate sources, and write and present 
clearly.
PACE = 15% of your final course grade
• Includes some assessments related to the Business Plan 
Competition, such as the teamwork contract.
