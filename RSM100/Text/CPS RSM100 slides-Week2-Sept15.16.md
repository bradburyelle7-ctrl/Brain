CareerLeader: Not just for Careers!
  In RSM100 and Your Team Projects
CareerLeader can help you reflect on:
• The types of work and tasks you enjoy most
• Skills you feel confident using
• Roles you may naturally gravitate toward in a team
• How your strengths can contribute to group projects
As you begin working in teams this term, these 
insights may help you better understand how you can 
contribute and collaborate effectively.
  As You Explore Your Future at Rotman Commerce
CareerLeader can also help you:
• Explore business career paths that align with your 
interests and motivators
• Connect potential career directions to Rotman Commerce 
specialist streams
• Make more informed decisions about courses, activities, 
and opportunities in the program
• Build greater confidence in your academic and career 
planning
Think of CareerLeader as a starting point for 
understanding yourself, not a test that tells you what to 
do.
