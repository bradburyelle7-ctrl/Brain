Physical planners
For a physical planner, you can purchase a structured
planner with built-in templates or a blank bullet journal to
customize the pages according to your needs!
Keep in mind that you may need to set aside a couple of
minutes every day to keep up with the physical planner.
Some students find it relaxing and enjoy this part of their
day, seeing it as a hobby, while others may find it too
time-consuming. It is completely a matter of personal
preference!
