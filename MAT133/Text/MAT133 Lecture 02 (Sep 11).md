Value of a Car
The value of a car decreases as the car gets older. Let’s consider the value
of this car,V, in thousands of dollars, as a function of the age of a car,a,
in years:V=f(a). The car owner hasn’t taken a calculus course before.
Poll Question
Which statement most accurately captures the plain english meaning of
“f(5) = 6”?
A The car is worth 5 thousand dollars less after 6 years.
B The car is worth 5 when it is 6 years old.
